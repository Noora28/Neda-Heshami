About

From Clinical Biochemistry to Neuroscience

I earned an MSc in Clinical Biochemistry at Hamadan University of Medical Sciences, where my thesis sat at the intersection of neurophysiology and biochemistry. I'm now a PhD candidate in Dr. Sarah McFarlane's lab at the University of Calgary and an NSERC BRAIN CREATE scholar.

Thesis title

Opsins in the pineal complex Regulates Melatonin Production

Using gene-edited Xenopus tadpoles, we showed pinopsin suppresses melatonin production in daylight, shaping skin pigmentation and daytime activity. We're looking for other opsins that may incorporate in melatonin regulation.
